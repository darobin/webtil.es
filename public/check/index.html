Does your tile host hold?

This loads a red-team tile through a loading server of your choosing. The tile tries every way out of the sandbox it knows: network egress over fetch, XHR, WebSocket, EventSource, beacons, WebRTC, external scripts, styles, images, fonts, frames and forms; scripting or navigating the host; reaching host runtime APIs; registering or unregistering its service worker; powerful features. It then reports what was contained and what got through, and why. Point it at your own load. domain to check a deployment.

The tile

What a result means

✓ contained is an escape the sandbox stopped; ✗ breach one that got through. ● permitted by policy marks what tiles are allowed to do on purpose (run their own code, keep storage on their throwaway origin, talk to the host over postMessage), and ▲ what they may do on a user gesture (open a window, as a link would). ? could not be told apart from inside the tile; ○ cannot be observed from inside at all (resource hints need a packet capture).

The tile's network probes aim at /probe/* on this site, which answers in ways no tile resource can, so the tile can tell "reached the network" from "the service worker answered with tile content". Nothing sensitive is sent. The tile and the probe endpoints are @dasl/containment-tile; the policy they test is in @dasl/tile-lexicon.
